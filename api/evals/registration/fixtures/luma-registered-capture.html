About Event

​Example Ventures and Stanford AI Club are excited to announce that Sam Example, Co-Founder and CEO of Northwind, will join us for our ’26–’27 Speaker Series!

​Sam co-founded Northwind in 2013 to make investing more accessible. Under his leadership, Northwind has grown into a public company with a current market capitalization of nearly $80 billion. The company generates $4.5 billion in net revenue a year. By the end of September 2026, Northwind had 28.7 million funded customers and $396 billion in assets on its platform.

​Sam is also Co-Founder and Executive Chairman of Example Labs, the AI company behind Example Prover. Example Prover uses Lean to verify mathematical proofs and performed at the gold medal level on the 2025 International Mathematical Olympiad problems. Sam holds a BS in Mathematics from Stanford and an MS in Mathematics from UCLA.

​This event is open to Stanford students and affiliates. You must RSVP to attend. It will take place at 5:15 p.m. on Wednesday, November 11, on Stanford’s campus.
